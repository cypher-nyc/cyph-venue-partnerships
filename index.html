what we bring to your space.

four primary IRL formats

01 · research hackathons

our platform is ed-tech made social — where the greatest thinkers wrestle with ideas before they wrestle with people. the experience on the platform is what we bring into your space.

02 · curated watch parties

films, tentpole moments, highly-watched sporting events, debates. what we host on our platform gets replicated in your space — strangers seated by productive tension, then strategically moved between groups throughout the night.

03 · meaningful discussions

on our platform, every discussion surfaces underground artifacts people would never have found otherwise. we’d love to surface your artifacts — books, records, prints, archives — into the hands of the people in the room.

04 · office hours

we work with niche experts — animal vets to offensive coordinators. different experts bring different audiences, and we’d love to bring that range into your space.
